About the Project

Stock Market Tracker is a real-time stock price tracker developed by a team of passionate students. Designed to reflect the dynamic nature of the stock market. Our aim was to build a simple, functional application powered by real-time data through an API. Which can reflect the data to the user according to their need and also give the information about the share market in a easier way.
